Heritage & Syncretism

Brotha Lok (Lộc) is a French music producer, composer, and arranger based in Toulouse, France. Rooted in hip-hop culture since 1998 through breakdancing and beatmaking, he has meticulously refined the art of sampling to create a powerful, genre-bending sonic signature.

Of White Tai (Thái Trắng) descent—an ethnic minority from Northern Vietnam—Brotha Lok’s artistic journey is deeply intertwined with a quest for cultural identity. While preceding generations focused on integration and assimilation, Brotha Lok operates in the realm of cultural syncretism and "post-memory". He gathers the fragmented heritage of his ancestors and welds them with raw, heavy-hitting underground hip-hop, constructing a musical bridge between East and West, past and present.

Craft & Recognition

A formally trained audio engineer with a Bachelor’s degree in Audio Production from IMAAT, his technical expertise matches his artistic vision. His groundbreaking work caught the attention of the acclaimed German label Saigon Supersound, which distributed his debut instrumental solo album, "Tru Lai: Flèches contre balles" (January 2024). The project received international critical acclaim, earning a prestigious nomination for the Preis der Deutschen Schallplattenkritik (PdSK) and receiving significant airplay on major national radio stations, including FIP (France) and Cosmo WDR (Germany).

Collaborations

Brotha Lok is also a key architect in the revival of Nhạc Vàng—the nostalgic and poetic popular Vietnamese music banned in 1975. His collaborative spirit has led to heavy-hitting projects with the Saigonese underground scene. Notably, his work during the 2019 Villa Saigon art residency with raw flow rapper Blacka birthed the hit single "Sai Gon Co Em", which amassed over 30 million views on YouTube after being featured on the hit TV show Rap Viet (Season 2). Whether flipping banned bolero vinyl samples into dusty boom-bap tracks or engineering organic fusion EPs, Brotha Lok stands as a unique, transnational force in modern music production—making ancestral memories dance to the beat of a heavy 90s kick.
