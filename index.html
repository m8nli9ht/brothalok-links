Artist Biography
龍

BROTHA LOK is a French music producer, composer, and arranger based in Toulouse, France. Rooted in Hip-Hop culture through breakdance and beatmaking since 1998, he earned a Bachelor's Degree in Audio Production from IMAAT in 2023.

Hailing from the White Tai (Thái Trắng) minority of Northern Vietnam, Brotha Lok's artistic signature lies in his deep identity quest: bridges built between the nostalgic, melancholic melodies of Nhạc Vàng (Vietnamese Golden Music/Bolero) and the rugged weight of Boom-Bap Hip-Hop.

"Not assimilation, but creative syncretism — weaving living roots from past to present, connecting Asia and the West."

Signed to the German revival label Saigon Supersound, his debut instrumental album « Trú Lại: Flèches contre balles » (2024) received a nomination for the prestigious Preis der Deutschen Schallplattenkritik (PdSK) and earned national radio airplay on France's FIP and Germany's Cosmo WDR.
