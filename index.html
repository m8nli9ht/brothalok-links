Heritage & Syncretism

Immersed in Hip-Hop culture since 1998 through breakdancing and beatmaking, Brotha Lok (from the Vietnamese given name Lộc) is a producer, composer, and arranger based in Toulouse, France[cite: 3]. Coming from the White Thai (Thái Trắng) ethnic minority of Northern Vietnam, his journey lies at the heart of a singular identity path: cultural syncretism[cite: 2, 3].

Where previous generations had to preserve or adapt their heritage, Brotha Lok engages in a process of creative reappropriation[cite: 2]. He gathers fragments of his dual identity to compose a unique contemporary aesthetic, where traditional ornaments dialogue with the urban energy of Boom-Bap[cite: 2].

The Art of Sampling & The Villa Saigon Residency

Sharpening his techniques during renowned battles—notably winning the Drop the Beat 2012 contest judged by DJ Logilo, Nodey, and Al'Tarba—he perfected a warm and textured production style[cite: 3]. Graduating with a Bachelor's Degree in Audio Production from IMAAT in 2023, he places organological research at the core of his creations[cite: 3].

In 2019, alongside Saigonese rapper Blacka, he participated in the Villa Saigon artist residency organized by the Institut Français[cite: 3]. Together, they composed 10 tracks crafted from rare samples of Nhạc Vàng—the popular Vietnamese Bolero-style music banned in 1975[cite: 3]. From these sessions came the flagship single "Sai Gon Co Em", which became an instant classic surpassing 30 million views on YouTube[cite: 3].

International Recognition & Saigon Supersound

His expertise in Nhạc Vàng led him naturally to join the prestigious German label Saigon Supersound[cite: 3]. In January 2024, he released his first solo instrumental album "Trủ Lại: Flèches contre balles", setting the Eurasian identity search to music[cite: 3]. The album met critical acclaim, receiving a nomination for the Preis der Deutschen Schallplattenkritik (PdSK) and regular airplay on FIP (France) and Cosmo WDR (Germany)[cite: 3].
